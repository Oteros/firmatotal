Add a visible signature to a PDF locally in your browser. Private, free and no account required.

Sign a PDF ↓
  1. 01

    Example: a three-page permission form

    Open the PDF and use the arrows to reach page 3, where the signature box appears. Create your signature, select “Use this signature” and add it to that page. Drag it into the box without covering any text. The first two pages need no extra mark if the recipient only asks you to sign at the end.

  2. 02

    Draw, type or choose an image

    Draw with a mouse or finger, type your name, or choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP image. A transparent PNG avoids covering the document. Typed names shrink to fit, including longer names. Check the mark before placing it: the same image is used for every position you add.

  3. 03

    Check the downloaded copy

    Choose the visual download and open the result in a PDF reader. Check all three pages, the name and the position. If no signature is placed, the download is a copy of the original. An image adds no certificate or proof of identity; check that the recipient accepts this kind of signature.

PAdES

An image is not a certificate

A drawn or typed signature is an image: it does not verify the signer's identity by itself. A certificate signature links the document to a key and lets a reader check for later changes. Acceptance depends on the recipient, certificate and applicable rules; PAdES format does not guarantee a qualified signature.
